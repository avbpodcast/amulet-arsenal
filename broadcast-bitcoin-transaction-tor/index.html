Amulet Broadcaster

Broadcast a signed Bitcoin transaction.

Amulet Broadcaster takes a transaction you have already signed and sends it to the Bitcoin network. It shows you exactly what the transaction does first, and makes you confirm four times before anything leaves your machine. It never needs your seed, and refuses one if you paste it by mistake.

For real funds: download, check the SHA-256, then run it from disk on an offline machine.

What it does

  • Accepts a signed PSBT (base64) or a raw transaction in hex, and detects which one you pasted.
  • Decodes it locally and shows every recipient address and amount before you send.
  • Offers 11 clearnet and 2 Tor broadcast endpoints, or your own Esplora-compatible node.
  • Keeps Tor and clearnet strictly apart, so a Tor session never falls back to the open internet.
  • Tracks the transaction after sending and shows when it is mined.

How to use it

  1. 1Paste the signed transactionThe tool starts offline and decodes it on your machine.
  2. 2Read what it doesCheck every address and amount. If anything looks wrong, stop here.
  3. 3Pick a node onlineThe tool goes online to check which broadcast nodes are reachable. Nothing is sent yet.
  4. 4Confirm and broadcast onlineOne final confirmation sends the transaction. This cannot be undone.

Steps marked "online" need the internet. Everything else runs offline.

Good to know

  • A broadcast is final. Once the network has the transaction, nobody can pull it back.
  • Tor endpoints only work when the page is opened in Tor Browser, or a browser routed through a Tor proxy.
  • The node you broadcast through can see your IP address and the transaction. Use Tor or your own node if that matters to you.

Questions

Why use a separate tool to broadcast?

It keeps signing and sending apart. The machine that holds your keys stays offline, and the online machine only ever sees a finished, signed transaction.

Can I broadcast a transaction signed in another wallet?

Yes. It accepts a signed PSBT or raw transaction hex from wallets such as Sparrow or Electrum.

Can the Broadcaster change my transaction?

No. A signed transaction cannot be altered without invalidating the signature. The tool only reads it and passes it on.
